01 01/2026 – 09/2026

Crazyflie-IsaacLab: RL-Tuned PID Gains for a Quadrotor

A Crazyflie 2.1 Brushless in Isaac Lab 3.0, flown by a four-layer PID cascade (position, velocity, attitude, rate). At every layer a small PPO network writes the 9 PID gains at every step; it cuts the tracking error of the hand-tuned PID by 23–62 %.

02 06/2025 – 12/2025

BalancingRobot-IsaacLab: Self-Balancing Robot with an RL Cascade

A Pololu Balboa 32U4 that balances and drives from a raw IMU and its wheel encoders, as the real robot would. A velocity network turns a speed and turn-rate command into wheel torques; a position network on top of it drives to a goal. No falls in 768 randomized episodes; goals reached within 8 mm (median).

  • Isaac Lab
  • PPO
  • ROS 2
  • IMU
  • Python

03 02/2025 – 05/2025

RotaryPendulum-IsaacLab: Learned Cascade PID

A Quanser QUBE-Servo 2 in Isaac Lab 3.0. A PPO policy writes the six gains of a two-loop PID at 100 Hz; the PID swings the pendulum up, balances it and turns the arm to commanded angles. Swing-up in 0.40 s (median) over 256 simulated robots.
